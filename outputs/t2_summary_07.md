# July outlook

**July Safety Snapshot – What to Expect in San Francisco’s Neighborhoods**

- **Tenderloin** – Leads the city with an average of **912.667 incidents** this month, including **166 persons** and **287 property** reports. It accounts for **0.104 share_of_city** and its seasonal boost is **1.149** times the baseline.  
- **Mission** – Close behind, the Mission sees **847.667 incidents** (‑ **149.667 persons**, **449 property**), a **0.096 share_of_city**, and a seasonal ratio of **1.032**.  
- **South of Market (SoMa)** – Registers **759.333 incidents** (‑ **116.333 persons**, **344.333 property**), **0.086 share_of_city**, with a seasonal factor of **1.055**.  

These three districts together represent the highest incident volumes for July.

---

- **Bayview‑Hunters Point** – **603 incidents** (‑ **83.333 persons**, **287.333 property**), **0.069 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.113**.  
- **Financial District/South Beach** – **536.333 incidents** (‑ **68.333 persons**, **365.667 property**), **0.061 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.074**.  

Both neighborhoods see a noticeable seasonal uptick but remain below the top‑three totals.

---

### Areas with the Largest Seasonal Increases
- **Noe Valley** – **135.667 incidents** (‑ **6.333 persons**, **104.667 property**), **0.015 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.241** (the strongest seasonal jump).  
- **Twin Peaks** – **52 incidents** (‑ **1.333 persons**, **38.667 property**), **0.006 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.215**.  
- **Marina** – **278.333 incidents** (‑ **15.333 persons**, **206.333 property**), **0.032 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.206**.  

If you’re visiting during July, these spots may feel a bit busier than their usual baseline.

---

### Quietest Neighborhoods (Fewest Incidents)
- **Presidio** – Only **6.667 incidents** (‑ **0 persons**, **5.667 property**), **0.001 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.039**.  
- **Seacliff** – **13 incidents** (‑ **0.333 persons**, **8.667 property**), **0.001 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.099**.  
- **Lincoln Park** – **14 incidents** (‑ **0 persons**, **12.333 property**), **0.002 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **0.908** (slightly below baseline).  
- **McLaren Park** – **15.667 incidents** (‑ **1.667 persons**, **7.333 property**), **0.002 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.259**.  
- **Treasure Island** – **32.667 incidents** (‑ **6.667 persons**, **14 property**), **0.004 share_of_city**, seasonal ratio **1.099**.  

These neighborhoods report the lowest incident counts for July and are generally the safest bets for a low‑key visit.

---

**Travel Tip:**  
If you’re prioritizing safety, aim for the quieter districts (Presidio, Seacliff, Lincoln Park, McLaren Park, Treasure Island). For nightlife and bustling urban vibes, the Tenderloin, Mission, and SoMa offer the most activity—but also the highest incident numbers, so stay alert and keep valuables secure.
