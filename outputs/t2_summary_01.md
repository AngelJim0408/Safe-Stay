# January outlook

**January Safety Snapshot – San Francisco Neighbourhoods**

- **Mission** leads the city with **836.333** average incidents (‑ 129 persons, 460.333 property), making up **0.102** of all city incidents; the seasonal ratio is **1.018**.  
- **Tenderloin** follows closely with **775.333** incidents (‑ 137.333 persons, 253.667 property), **0.095** share of the city and a seasonal ratio of **0.976**.  
- **South of Market** records **685.0** incidents (‑ 110 persons, 328 property), **0.084** of the city total, seasonal ratio **0.952**.  
- **Bayview‑Hunters Point** sees **568.667** incidents (‑ 64.667 persons, 273 property), **0.069** share, seasonal ratio **1.049**.  
- **Financial District/South Beach** has **473.667** incidents (‑ 45.333 persons, 333.667 property), **0.058** share, seasonal ratio **0.948**.  

**Neighbourhoods with the sharpest seasonal upticks**  
- **Twin Peaks**: **52.333** incidents (‑ 3 persons, 38 property), **0.006** city share, seasonal ratio **1.223**.  
- **Portola**: **126.667** incidents (‑ 20.333 persons, 72.333 property), **0.015** share, seasonal ratio **1.213**.  
- **North Beach**: **319.667** incidents (‑ 26.333 persons, 252.333 property), **0.039** share, seasonal ratio **1.144**.  

**Neighbourhoods with the fewest incidents**  
- **Presidio**: **8.667** incidents (‑ 0.333 persons, 6 property), **0.001** share, seasonal ratio **1.351**.  
- **McLaren Park**: **10.0** incidents (‑ 1.667 persons, 4.333 property), **0.001** share, seasonal ratio **0.804**.  
- **Seacliff**: **11.333** incidents (‑ 0.333 persons, 9.667 property), **0.001** share, seasonal ratio **0.958**.  
- **Lincoln Park**: **23.667** incidents (‑ 0.333 persons, 21 property), **0.003** share, seasonal ratio **1.535**.  
- **Treasure Island**: **27.333** incidents (‑ 6.333 persons, 11.333 property), **0.003** share, seasonal ratio **0.92**.  

**What this means for you:**  
- The Mission, Tenderloin, and SoMa have the highest incident volumes in January, so stay alert, especially in crowded spots.  
- Areas like Twin Peaks, Portola, and North Beach see the strongest seasonal increases, so extra caution is wise if you’re visiting during this month.  
- Presidio, McLaren Park, Seacliff, Lincoln Park, and Treasure Island report the lowest incident counts, offering comparatively calmer environments.  

Travel safely and enjoy the city!
